Scanner rules and troubleshooting
  • LONG: 30m EMA20 above EMA50, recent bullish cross, retest in the previous 1–4 completed candles, and strong bullish confirmation.
  • SHORT: Opposite EMA cross, recent retest and strong bearish confirmation.
  • 1H trend: LONG requires 1H close above a rising EMA50. SHORT requires 1H close below a falling EMA50.
  • Sideways filter: ADX(14) must be at least 18 and EMA20/EMA50 separation at least 0.08%.
  • Volume: Current completed 30m candle volume must be at least 1.5× the previous 20 completed candles' average.
  • Trade plan: Entry is the confirmation candle close. SL is beyond the retest swing with a 0.20 ATR buffer. TP1 = 1.5R and TP2 = 2.5R.
  • HOT: Relative 24h turnover and/or absolute price movement ranks near the active end of the currently loaded market.
  • Chart: TradingView links open the matching MEXC USDT perpetual symbol at the 30-minute interval.
