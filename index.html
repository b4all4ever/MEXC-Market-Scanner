Scanner rules and troubleshooting
  • LONG: last completed 30m candle has EMA20 above EMA50; recent bullish cross is within the last 6 completed candles; after the cross, price revisits the EMA20/EMA50 area and a completed candle closes bullish above EMA20.
  • SHORT: opposite conditions, including a bearish close below EMA20.
  • HOT: relative 24h turnover and/or price movement ranks near the active end of the currently loaded market.
  • Retest tolerances are designed as a practical starting point; signals can be scarce. HOT is a relative ranking, not a prediction.
  • If data does not load, your network or region may block the MEXC API. This is a browser-only site, so it cannot bypass API restrictions. Try another network or deploy a small backend proxy on a free tier.
